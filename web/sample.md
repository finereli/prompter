Most founders think their problem is marketing. They write more posts, buy more ads, and try a new channel every month. But when nobody responds, the problem is usually earlier than that.

The problem is positioning. If you can't say in one sentence what your product is, who it's for, and why they should care right now, no amount of marketing will fix it. You'll just be spending money to confuse more people.

Here's a simple test. Ask five customers to describe what you do to a friend. If their answers don't match each other, or don't match yours, you have a positioning problem.

The good news is that positioning is cheap to fix. It doesn't take a new product or a bigger budget. It takes a few honest conversations and the willingness to throw away the description you're attached to.
